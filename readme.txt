This data accompanies Laing et al. 2025 "Quantifying movement: expanding the ichnologist toolkit". 

Folders beginning with "Csemi" relate to the data gathered from specimens of Cruziana semiplicata.

Files are named (e.g. Csemi_Spain_31_discretized.mat) according to the ichnospecies name (Csemi), locality (Spain), and specimen number (31). After which information on the analytical step follows (discretized).

Folders contain the following:

A_PathArtboards = Images for the first half of "Step A: Discretization". Includes photographs of C. semiplicata from which individual specimens were traced in Adobe Illustrator, including their specimen ID, and the traced individual specimen paths used in subsequent steps. 

A_DiscretizedPaths = MATLAB files for the second half of "Step A: Discretization". Contains x,y data of individual C. semiplicata specimens. 

B_SegmentedPaths = MATLAB files for "Step B: Segmentation". Contains x,y data of individual C. semiplicata specimens collected at a segment distance multiplier of 0.5.

C_TurningAngleData = MATLAB files for "Step C: Turning Angle". Contains segmented x,y data (step B) and the corresponding turning angle data.
